---
name: Milestone review rhythm
description: The repeating 3-day and 6-day checkpoint rule used by vocabulary progression.
---

Each review checkpoint is identified by both its review size and the completed-day boundary, such as a 3-day checkpoint at day 9 or a 6-day checkpoint at day 12. At six-day boundaries, only the comprehensive six-day checkpoint is mandatory; the three-day card remains available as optional practice.

**Why:** A single completion flag per review type would stop the rhythm after the first review and could not distinguish later checkpoints.

**How to apply:** Find the first contiguous completed-day boundary without its keyed checkpoint. Keep later daily sets locked until that checkpoint is complete, and generate the review mix from the most recent contiguous completed days.
